Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, and payment information when you create an account or place an order.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process your orders, send you order confirmations and status updates, improve our services, communicate with you about promotions and campus events, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with canteen staff to fulfill your orders.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You can update your profile information directly in the app or contact us at support@canteen.edu.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@canteen.edu or visit our Contact page.
